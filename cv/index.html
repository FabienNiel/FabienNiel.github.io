Professional Summary

Research scientist with experience building and evaluating foundation-model training systems at Mistral AI and developing a production ML system from first implementation through prospective multi-hospital validation at Quantiq. PhD in theoretical physics, with expertise in statistical modelling, noisy time-series data, numerical simulation, and scientific computing.

    Co-founder & CTO
    Quantiq
    • Built Quantiq’s end-to-end camera-based physiological sensing system from scratch, extracting clean photoplethysmographic signals and estimating heart and respiratory rates in real time from commodity webcams and smartphone cameras under uncontrolled lighting, motion, and device conditions.
    • Designed the data acquisition and evaluation infrastructure for 10,000+ participants (300+ hours of synchronized video and medical-reference signals) collected across 5+ devices, 20+ environments, and six hospitals.

    • Brought the system from first implementation to a CE-marked production medical device, leading technical direction across an eight-person team; achieved prospective RMSEs of 3.1 bpm for heart rate and 2.9 breaths/min for respiratory rate.
